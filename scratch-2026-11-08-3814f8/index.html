An Alexandria brokerage that would rather show you three right homes than thirty wrong ones.

4A Real Estate is a full service property partner. We help clients buy, sell, rent and invest across Alexandria, the North Coast and Cairo, and we stay with each client from the first viewing to the handover of keys.

Every unit we recommend is visited by our team, its contract reviewed, and its price checked against recent sales in the same project. We work directly with the developers behind those projects, so our clients hear about new phases first.
